CLOSED-LOOP CIRCULAR LIFECYCLE

From farm byproduct to industrial asset.

AgriCycle eliminates the guesswork from agricultural waste exchange with automated geolocation, quality assessment, and transparent order tracking.

STAGE 01
🌾

1. List Crop Biomass

Farmers submit crop type, available kilograms, location coordinates, and select sell, barter, or free exchange.

STAGE 02
🔬

2. AI Quality Check

Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) analyzes fiber purity and moisture levels to assign verified Grade A/B certifications.

STAGE 03
📍

3. Haversine Matching

Our geospatial algorithm calculates geodesic distances to connect farmers with nearest bio-industries and compost units.

STAGE 04
🚚

4. Dispatch & Reuse

Utilizer places request, pickup logistics are arranged, and the exchange receipt generates verified carbon credits.
