🎯 SDG 12: Responsible Consumption

Promotes closed-loop resource cycles by converting agricultural waste into industrial feedstocks, bio-plastics, and mushroom beds.

Empowers smallholder farmers in Karnataka with secondary income channels through peer-to-peer barter and sales.

Restores soil organic carbon and microbial health by repurposing organic residues back into compost and bio-char.
